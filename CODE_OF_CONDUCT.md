# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Critique the code, not the person.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space,
and to anyone taking part — contributors and maintainers alike.

## Expected

- Give and take feedback about the work without making it personal
- Assume the other person is trying to help until shown otherwise
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change; scope decisions are real
  decisions, not rejections of you
- Say when you are unsure, and say when you are wrong

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussion

## Technical disagreement is welcome

Arguing that a benchmark is unsound, a claim is overstated, or an approach is
wrong is *the point of an open project* — several claims in this repository were
corrected exactly that way. Bring evidence, and hold others' arguments to the
same standard you would want applied to your own.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening a GitHub issue, or privately by emailing
<arunvpp24@gmail.com> if a public issue is not appropriate.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments and code that breach this
document, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they
consider inappropriate. Reports will be handled discreetly.
